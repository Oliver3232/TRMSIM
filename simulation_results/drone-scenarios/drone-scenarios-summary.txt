TRMSim-WSN Drone Scenario Batch Results
========================================
Mode: --headless-batch-scenario
Models evaluated: EigenTrust, PeerTrust, BTRM_WSN, LFTM

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario: drone-swarm  (Drone Swarm -- Reconnaissance)
  Nodes: 30  |  Networks: 15  |  Executions per network: 40
  Topology: dynamic + oscillating  |  Malicious: 5%  |  Relay: 40%

  EigenTrust :  4 578 ms
  LFTM       :  3 712 ms
  BTRM_WSN   :    724 ms
  PeerTrust  :    189 ms

  Recommended model: LFTM
  Notes: EigenTrust cost dominated by matrix computation per execution.
         LFTM close behind due to repeated fuzzy re-evaluation on membership
         changes. BTRM_WSN and PeerTrust well under 1 second.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario: drone-logistics  (Drone Logistics -- Delivery Fleet)
  Nodes: 200  |  Networks: 5  |  Executions per network: 20
  Topology: static  |  Malicious: 7%  |  Relay: 65%

  EigenTrust : 55 318 ms
  LFTM       :  8 146 ms
  BTRM_WSN   :  2 329 ms
  PeerTrust  :    557 ms

  Recommended model: PeerTrust
  Notes: Largest network of the three scenarios. EigenTrust overhead
         dominated by algorithmic complexity (matrix over 200 nodes), not
         topology dynamics -- static topology does not reduce its cost.
         PeerTrust benefits from stable repeated interactions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario: drone-contested  (Drone Contested -- Hostile Airspace)
  Nodes: 80  |  Networks: 10  |  Executions per network: 45
  Topology: oscillating + collusion  |  Malicious: 38%  |  Relay: 50%

  EigenTrust : 45 961 ms
  LFTM       :  6 113 ms
  BTRM_WSN   :  5 393 ms
  PeerTrust  :    637 ms

  Recommended model: EigenTrust
  Notes: Highest malicious proportion and collusion force EigenTrust to
         re-propagate global trust vector aggressively. BTRM_WSN and LFTM
         comparable. PeerTrust remains fast but offers weaker guarantees
         under coordinated false recommendations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cross-scenario observations:

  PeerTrust  -- consistently fastest across all scenarios (189--637 ms)
  EigenTrust -- consistently most expensive (4 578--55 318 ms)
  BTRM_WSN   -- middle ground, more sensitive to oscillation than node count
  LFTM       -- middle ground, higher cost than BTRM_WSN in oscillating cases
